ScholarGauge Android app – Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Developer: KStats Lab (kstatslab@gmail.com)

ScholarGauge has no server and collects nothing about you. The app shows journal quartiles, citation facts, topics and novelty checks from open scholarly databases. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Your choice

On first start the app shows what it sends and to whom. Nothing is looked up online until you choose Agree and start. You can turn look-ups off at any time in Settings. The journal checker works without internet.

What it sends, and to whom

InformationSent toPurpose
A researcher's name, ORCID iD or OpenAlex id that you type; the author profiles you pickOpenAlex (api.openalex.org)Find the author and their works
DOIs or titles of papers you paste or check, with the first author's surname when knownCrossref (api.crossref.org) and OpenAlexFind each paper's record

Your OpenAlex API key, only if you enter oneOpenAlexLarger daily allowance
DOIs of a profile's papers and your Web of Science key – only if you add that keyClarivate (api.clarivate.com)Count the papers in the Web of Science Core Collection

Requests name the app and its contact address (as Crossref and OpenAlex ask), never you. All requests use HTTPS and go directly from your phone to these services. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no tracking, profiling, or sale or transfer of data.

What it keeps

Your settings, the profiles you opened recently, papers you marked as "not this author's", any SCImago files you import and cached look-ups are kept only in the app's storage on your phone. Settings → Clear cache removes the saved look-ups; clearing the app's storage in the phone's settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything. The app does not use Android's cloud backup for this data.

Permissions

Internet only. The app does not use the camera, contacts, location, microphone or files, except a file you choose yourself to import, or a CSV the app saves to your Downloads folder when you ask for it.

Third-party services

OpenAlex, Crossref and Clarivate have their own terms and privacy policies. The journal data (SCImago Journal & Country Rank) is built into the app; no request is made to SCImago. Links to papers or to SCImago open in your phone's browser. ScholarGauge is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, SCImago, Elsevier, Clarivate, Crossref or OpenAlex.

Children

The app is a research tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

Any change to these practices will be described here with a new date before it takes effect.

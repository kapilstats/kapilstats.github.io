Research

My research develops classical and Bayesian methods for lifetime data, with a focus on situations where complete data are not available because of censoring. Most of this work is done with my PhD students and collaborators; the full list is on the Publications page.

Censored data and censoring schemes

Life tests are often stopped before every unit has failed, to save time and cost, and in medical studies patients may leave before the event of interest is observed. I study estimation and prediction under many censoring schemes: progressive Type-II, progressive first-failure, hybrid and unified hybrid, adaptive progressive and adaptive progressive hybrid, joint and balanced joint progressive censoring for two populations, block progressive censoring, and random censoring (including block random and progressive random censoring).

Stress-strength reliability

Inference for \(R = P(Y < X)\), the probability that a component’s strength \(X\) exceeds the stress \(Y\) applied to it, and for multicomponent stress-strength systems, in which a system works when at least \(s\) of its \(k\) components withstand the common stress.

Bayesian inference

Bayesian estimation of parameters, reliability characteristics and entropy under different loss functions, using Markov chain Monte Carlo methods and approximation methods such as those of Lindley and Tierney–Kadane, with highest posterior density (HPD) credible intervals. Bayesian results are compared with maximum likelihood estimates and confidence intervals through simulation studies and real data.

  • Kumar, K., Kumar, I., and Ng, H. K. T. (2024). On estimation of Shannon’s entropy of Maxwell distribution based on progressively first-failure censored data. Stats. DOI
  • Kumar, K., and Kumari, A. (2023). Bayesian and likelihood estimation in two inverse Pareto populations under joint progressive censoring. Journal of the Indian Society for Probability and Statistics. DOI

Lifetime distributions

Properties, estimation and applications of lifetime models, including the Lindley and power Lindley, generalized inverted exponential, inverse Pareto and inverted exponentiated Pareto, Maxwell, Nakagami, log-logistic, inverse Weibull, inverse Rayleigh, Burr Type XII, xgamma, generalized half logistic and generalized gamma distributions, and the Weibull-G family.

Reliability of repairable systems

Stochastic modelling of industrial systems, such as a turbogenerator unit of a thermal power plant and a ready-mix cement plant, with reliability characteristics estimated under classical and Bayesian frameworks.

  • Chaudhary, R., Saini, M., Kumar, A., and Kumar, K. (2025). Reliability characteristics analysis of ready-mix cement plant under classical and Bayesian inferential framework: a comparative analysis. Brazilian Journal of Biometrics. DOI

Attendance Register — Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 October 2026 · App: Attendance Register (हाज़िरी रजिस्टर), package io.github.kapilstats.attendance · Developer: KStats Lab

English

Attendance Register does not collect, send or share any information about you or your students.

Where the data is kept

Class names, student names, roll numbers and attendance marks that you enter are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to a server, to the developer or to any other company.

Backups on your phone and in your own Google account

Two kinds of backup copy of the register may exist, and both stay under your control:

  • Daily file on the phone. When you leave the app, it saves one file a day (the newest 7 are kept) in the Downloads folder, inside a folder named AttendanceRegister (on Android 9 or older, in a folder you choose). It lets you recover the register if the app is removed. The file contains your class names, student names, roll numbers and attendance. Other apps that you allow to read your files can see these folders, so delete the files if you do not want them.
  • Android's own Google backup. If Google backup is switched on in your phone's Settings, Android may copy the app's register file to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. The app asks for this copy to be made only when it can be encrypted with your phone's screen lock, so neither Google nor the developer can read it. This is done by Android, not by this app; the app itself never sends anything and the developer cannot see it. You can switch it off in your phone's Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login or registration.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or other phone data.

Files you choose to create or share

When you import a student list, the app reads only the file you pick. When you make a report (PDF, Excel, CSV), a backup file, or share the shortage list, the file or text is created on your phone and goes only where you send it (for example a folder, WhatsApp or email). What happens to it after that is decided by you and the app you send it to.

Deleting data

You can delete a student, a lecture or a whole class inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the phone (the daily copy files in Downloads and any copy in your Google backup stay until you delete them). Keep a backup file if you may need the register later.

Students' information

The app is meant for a teacher's own register. You are responsible for handling your students' information according to your institution's rules. Because nothing leaves the phone unless you share it, keep the phone locked and share reports only with the people who should see them.

Children

The app is for teachers. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: KStats Lab (Kapil Kumar) · kapilstatslab@gmail.com

कक्षा, विद्यार्थियों के नाम, रोल नंबर और हाज़िरी सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में रहती है। ऐप के पास इंटरनेट की अनुमति ही नहीं है। कोई खाता, विज्ञापन या ट्रैकिंग नहीं है। रिपोर्ट, बैकअप या कमी की सूची सिर्फ़ वहीं जाती है जहाँ आप ख़ुद भेजें।

बैकअप: ऐप बंद करते समय रोज़ एक फ़ाइल Downloads → AttendanceRegister में सहेजी जाती है (Android 9 या पुराने में आपका चुना फ़ोल्डर); पिछली 7 रखी जाती हैं। फ़ोन की सेटिंग में Google बैकअप चालू हो तो Android यह रजिस्टर आपके अपने Google खाते में भी रख सकता है, पर तभी जब वह फ़ोन के स्क्रीन लॉक से एन्क्रिप्ट हो सके, इसलिए Google या डेवलपर इसे पढ़ नहीं सकते। यह Android करता है, ऐप ख़ुद कुछ नहीं भेजता। ज़रूरत न हो तो ये फ़ाइलें हटा दें या Google बैकअप बंद कर दें।

ऐप हटाने पर उसका सारा डेटा फ़ोन से मिट जाता है (Downloads की कॉपी और Google बैकअप की कॉपी आपके हटाने तक रहती हैं)। सवाल: kapilstatslab@gmail.com

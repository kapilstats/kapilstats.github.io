ScholarGauge

Journal quartiles, novelty and research profiles for Google Scholar™ – any field

Add to Chrome (link after publication)

Under every Google Scholar result

  • The journal's SCImago quartile and SJR today, and its quartile in the year the paper appeared (1999–2025)
  • Warnings for retracted papers and for journals dropped by Scopus
  • Citations per year and the paper's citation percentile in its field
  • The paper's OpenAlex topic and its UN Sustainable Development Goals
  • For reliability and lifetime-model papers: distribution, censoring scheme, model and method
  • Sort, filter by quartile, export to CSV

On a Scholar profile

A research fingerprint, for all years or recent years: a quartile matrix (papers in Q1–Q4 journals now and when published, and which journals moved up or down), how many papers are in Scopus-covered journals and – with your own free Clarivate key – in the Web of Science Core Collection, papers per year by quartile when published, venues, citations, research areas, UN SDGs, co-authors and countries, interdisciplinarity (Rao–Stirling), exploration of new topics, and a table of variety (diversity) indices – Shannon, Simpson, evenness – for topics, fields, keywords, journals, co-authors and SDGs. Point at any number for its meaning.

Research lab

  • Topic map – which keywords the papers on a topic study together, and which common pairs are missing (gaps)
  • Has it been done? – papers combining your ideas, with a novelty (gap) index from 0 (crowded) to 100 (open gap)

Privacy

Free, no account, no server, no analytics. Nothing is looked up until you agree. Only paper titles, first-author surnames, DOIs and your Research-lab searches are sent, and only to Crossref and OpenAlex (and, only if you add your own Web of Science key, the DOIs of a profile's papers to Clarivate).
